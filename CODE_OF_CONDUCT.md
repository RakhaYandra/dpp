# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful and productive place for everyone who contributes or uses it, regardless of experience, background, identity, location or role. People of every level, from first-time contributors to experienced practitioners, make this framework better.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent; ask questions when something is unclear.
- Give feedback on **work, not on people**; be specific and constructive.
- Accept feedback graciously; disagree openly but without hostility.
- Credit others' ideas and work.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
- Welcome newcomers and explain jargon.
- Use inclusive, plain language.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, threats, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sexualised language or attention, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.
- Submitting content you do not have the right to share (for example copied from licensed or confidential sources).
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainers at **[conduct contact placeholder]**. Reports are handled promptly, confidentially and fairly. Maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith, and anyone involved in a report who has a conflict of interest will step aside from handling it.

**Typical responses** (adjusted to the situation): a private clarification; a public or private warning; a temporary restriction; a permanent removal from project spaces.

## Attribution and Alternatives

This is a plain-language code of conduct written for this project. Projects that prefer a widely adopted, community-maintained text can adopt the Contributor Covenant instead; replace this file with it and keep a contact for reports.
