> **Guidance:** Every number needs a **source and date**, or a visible calculation. Prefer **bottom-up** sizing (number of target customers × realistic annual value) over top-down claims ("if we get 1% of a huge market"). Do not invent statistics: if you cannot find a figure, write "Unknown" and state how you will estimate it.
