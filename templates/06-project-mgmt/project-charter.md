| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Authorizes the project and sets its boundaries: why it exists, what it will deliver, who decides, what it may spend and how long it may take. | **When to use** | After the Product Brief and goals, when committing people or money. Solo projects may use a half-page version: objectives, constraints, decision rules. |
| **Owner** | Project Manager (solo: founder). Approved by the sponsor. |
