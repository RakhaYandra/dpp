> **Guidance:** Start from **decisions and needs**, not from meetings. For each audience ask: what do they need to decide or do, and how soon must they know? Use the lightest channel that works. Bad news travels fastest through a defined path.

## 1. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence (H/M/L) | Needs to know | Preferred channel | Owner of the relationship |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Return, risk | High | Progress, budget, decisions needed | Short monthly report | PM |
| Pilot customers | Early access | Medium | Release timing, known issues | E-mail | PM |

| Decision requests | Decision maker | Get a decision | Written | As needed | | Option summary + recommendation |
| Release notices | Team, support | Readiness | Chat/e-mail | Per release | PM | See Release Plan |
