# Product Brief: SlotWise

> *Worked example. SlotWise is a fictional product. Every fact here is invented for illustration.*

## Problem

Independent salons and studios (1–5 staff) take bookings by phone and messaging apps. Owners lose time answering, double-book, and lose revenue to no-shows. In a survey of 24 owners in the pilot region, the median no-show rate was 18% of appointments and owners spent about 6 hours a week on scheduling messages.

**Evidence:** 24 owner interviews and survey (Aug 2026); booking logs from 3 pilot salons (1,140 appointments).

## Who it is for

PER-01 Maya, the owner of a small salon (see [PRD](../03-requirements/prd.md)).

## Proposed solution

A web booking page the salon shares by link, with live availability, automatic confirmations and reminders, and a simple calendar for staff. Success is defined in [Goals & Metrics](../02-strategy/goals-and-metrics.md).

## Out of scope for this brief

Payments, marketplace discovery, multi-location chains.
